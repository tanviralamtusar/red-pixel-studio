Our story

A small team

Red Pixels began with two cameras, one borrowed room and a belief that good production should feel human.

Today, we're a multidisciplinary studio working across photography, film and creative production. We partner with ambitious people and brands to find the thing that makes their story singular — then we make it impossible to miss.

We're curious collaborators, calm on set and particular about the details that make an image feel alive.
